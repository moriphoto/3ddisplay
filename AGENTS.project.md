WorldEntire Project Instructions & Rules
Core Positioning (Always Lead With This)

We are not selling “another display system”.
We are establishing a new category of foldable large-format display hardware that delivers measurable commercial, operational, and environmental benefits across the print supply chain.
Use this positioning in all messaging, campaigns, and content.

Brand Voice & Tone

Precise, intelligent, and practical.
Confident but helpful — never pushy.
Speak like a forward-thinking engineer who genuinely wants customers to succeed.
Emphasise quality, simplicity, and real-world performance.

Content & Campaign Rules

Prefer hybrid approach: Combine real photography + AI/CGI for mockups and storytelling.
Use interesting or narrative-driven locations for visual impact (e.g. poppy fields, forests, abstract environments) even if not practical in real life — these are for brand storytelling.
Create short, simple process videos (idea → computer → print).
Educate gently on file quality and DPI without overwhelming the customer.

DPI & Print Quality

Default recommendation: 300 DPI is more than enough for most exhibition use.
Offer 2400 DPI for fine art / high-detail work where people view prints up close.
Use the 5x4" vs 35mm film analogy to explain why higher DPI matters.

WEfold Product Reality

Best suited for indoor / commercial environments (exhibitions, shopping centres, offices, events).
Not ideal for long-term outdoor use (wind + direct sunlight issues).

Future App Vision (Reference When Creating Content)

The app should have three sections: Discover (info/pricing), Order (self-serve), Create (AI-assisted).
Prioritise a conversational AI agent that guides users naturally rather than rigid menus.
Support two routes: customers with ready artwork + customers who need design help.

AI Usage Rules

AI is a powerful tool for speed and ideation.
Always maintain authenticity — the final product must feel real and buyable.
Test with tonal/greyscale physical boards when needed to improve AI mockup accuracy.
Mix real footage with AI where it saves time and money without misleading the customer.

General Working Rules

Keep everything clean, precise, and professional.
Structure documents clearly (use headings, tables, bullet points).
Save all important CEO quotes and ideas in the Creative Hub under 01_Brand_Foundations.
Always tie new ideas back to the core positioning.
